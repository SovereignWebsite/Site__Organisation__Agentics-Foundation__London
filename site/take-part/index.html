How to take part

The links below are the ones the chapter's own site, london.agentics.org, gives (read 24 November 2026).

Come to an event

Meetups run every second Wednesday, online and in person, and public meetups are free. Sign up for each one on the chapter's Luma calendar.

The hackathon will be listed there too: what we know so far.

Join the chapter

There is no membership fee. Join with the chapter's membership form.

Follow and talk

  • Discord: the Agentics Foundation community.
  • LinkedIn: the Agentics Foundation.
  • YouTube: the London chapter's recordings.
  • community.agentics.org: the foundation's community site.

Workshops, talks and partnerships

For a workshop, a talk or a partnership, use the "Get Involved" section of london.agentics.org.
