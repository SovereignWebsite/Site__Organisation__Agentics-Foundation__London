About the chapter

Agentics London

Agentics London is the London (UK) chapter of the global Agentics Foundation. Everything on this page is from the chapter's own site, london.agentics.org, read 24 November 2026.

What it is

A community of engineers, product people, strategists and educators focused on turning agentic AI into practical adoption through hands-on, face-to-face learning. Public meetups are free; paid workshops and strategy sprints cover venues, travel and community projects.

Chapter leaders

The chapter was founded by UK ambassadors Bence Csernak and Mondweep Chakravorty with four other chapter leaders. Its site names six co-founders:

  • Bence Csernak, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)
  • Mondweep Chakravorty, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)
  • Mark De Rijk, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)
  • Chris Poulter, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)
  • Rocky Verma, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)
  • Vincent Sider, Chapter Co-Founder (LinkedIn)

How it works

  • Lean and agile: experiments first; most activities run under members' existing companies.
  • Open tooling: code on GitHub, and meeting artefacts shared with participants.
  • Sustainable revenue: workshops and sprints are paid; public meetups stay free.

The Agentics Foundation

The chapter is part of the global Agentics Foundation, founded by Reuven Cohen. The foundation's projects are on GitHub.
