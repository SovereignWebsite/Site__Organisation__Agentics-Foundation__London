The London hackathon

A hackathon with the London chapter of the Agentics Foundation, showing examples of Sovereign Websites and AgentiCCells.

Draft. The chapter and Dinis Cruz agreed this hackathon on a call on 24 November 2026. Details marked "To be confirmed" have not been published yet.

What we know

  • It is planned for the next few weeks, after a test run.
  • It shows examples of Sovereign Websites: websites whose owners hold the files, the history and the address, and can move them anywhere.
