Design rounds

Designs for this site

Each design is the same content, the home page and the hackathon page, styled a different way. The owner picks one; the pages that are live today keep the plain seed style until then.

All designs load nothing from anywhere else: they use the visitor's own fonts, except d7, whose open-licence fonts are served from this site. None uses the chapter's logo or branding. Each passes the accessibility check (axe) in light and dark mode. Designs d2 to d9 were checked in both.

Round 4 (24 November 2026): agent-first

Designs built around agents and the agentic workflow, after a look at how the Agentics Foundation presents itself at agentics.org and london.agentics.org.

  • d7, Swarm (hackathon page): in step with the Foundation's own look: warm cream, coral, condensed headings over a monospace body, the terminal window and the [ ] brackets, with a live agent network behind the hero. Its two open-licence typefaces are served from this site. No logo or wordmark: whether to align this closely is the chapter's call.
  • d8, Workflow (hackathon page): the page is an agent run. The hero shows the hackathon as a live trace, the chapter is a pipeline of steps, and every detail still to be confirmed is a pending step rather than a gap.
  • d9, Mission control (hackathon page): the chapter as a dashboard: the hackathon is the mission, each figure a tile with its source, and one panel shows the real agents and checks behind this site.

Round 3

  • d6, Signal (hackathon page): Poster's dark hero and lime, the Swiss grid's numbered sections, and monospace for dates and every "to be confirmed". Round 3 tightened the spacing, gave each gap its own labelled box, and fixed the numbers in dark mode. The keeper's recommendation before round 4.
  • d4, Swiss grid (hackathon page): Round 3 removed the empty rows under each section. The calmest and most readable option.

Round 2

  • Took d2, d4 and a new hybrid, d6, forward. Fixed in all: the design banner now sits in a landmark; the source notes are one size.
  • d2, Poster (hackathon page): the hero button's text was white on lime (failed contrast): fixed; the top bar no longer runs under the Sovereign Website menu.

Round 1: five directions

  • d1, Editorial (hackathon page): a printed programme: serif, warm paper, rules. Calm, but reads as a literary magazine more than a hackathon. Not taken forward.
  • d2, Poster (hackathon page): a dark event poster with very large type and one lime accent. The most energy.
  • d3, Terminal (hackathon page): an engineer's console in monospace. On topic, but long text is tiring to read. Its monospace data labels live on in d6.
  • d4, Swiss grid (hackathon page): a strict grid with numbered sections and one red. The clearest structure.
  • d5, Community (hackathon page): soft colours and rounded cards. Friendly, but generic. Not taken forward.
